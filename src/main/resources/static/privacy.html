Privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

The Academy Portal is the academy's internal record system for student details, faculty details, timetables and certificates. This page explains what the portal stores, why it stores it and who can see it.

Information the portal holds

  • Account details: full name, username, academy email address and role (administrator, teacher or student).
  • Student records: roll number, programme and section, admission date, status, student and guardian contact details, and subject enrolments.
  • Faculty records: employee code, department, designation, subjects taught, office room and contact details.
  • Timetable: classes, rooms, faculty and weekly session times.
  • Profile picture: only when the person or an administrator has uploaded one.

Why it is used

The information is used to run the academy: keeping the student register current, publishing timetables, putting teachers in touch with the classes they teach, and preparing graduation certificates. The portal does not use the information for advertising, and the academy does not sell it.

Who can see it

  • Administrators can see and edit every record.
  • Teachers can see the student directory, timetables and the contact directory.
  • Students can see their own profile, their own timetable, their own subjects and the faculty directory.
  • Profile pictures are visible to every signed-in user.
  • Contact lists are limited by role: teachers see the students in their classes, and students see the faculty who teach them together with the administration mailbox.

Cookies and sessions

The portal sets a session cookie (JSESSIONID) so a signed-in person stays signed in, and a CSRF cookie (XSRF-TOKEN) that protects forms against cross-site request forgery. It does not use advertising cookies, analytics scripts or third-party trackers.

Storage and security

Records are held in the academy's database server, which is operated by the academy. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, so nobody at the academy can read them back. Access requires a signed-in account and ends when the session expires or the account is disabled.

Retention and removal

Student and faculty records are kept for as long as the academy needs them for its register and its legal obligations. Removing a student or teacher also removes their enrolments, timetable sessions and sign-in account. Removing a profile picture deletes the stored image.

Your choices

  • Correct your contact details through the portal, or ask the academy office to correct them.
  • Change your password under Account settings.
  • Remove your profile picture under Account settings.
  • Ask the academy office about access to or removal of your record.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.

Contact

Questions about the portal or the information it holds can go to the academy office at office@academy.edu or on +91 80 4000 1010.
