Using the portal

The Academy Portal is provided by the academy for its own administration. By signing in you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, do not use the portal.

Accounts

  • Accounts are issued by the academy office, and one account belongs to one person.
  • Keep your password private. Do not share your account or leave a signed-in session open for someone else.
  • If you are given a default password, change it from Account settings after your first sign-in.
  • Tell the academy office at once if you think someone else knows your password or has used your account.

Acceptable use

  • Use the portal only for academy work: student records, faculty records, timetables, contacts and certificates.
  • Do not try to open records, pages or actions that your role does not allow.
  • Do not interfere with the portal, probe its security or attempt to access it without authorisation.
  • Do not export or pass on personal information except as part of your academy duties.

Records

Records in the portal belong to the academy. Staff are responsible for keeping the records they manage accurate. Students and teachers can ask the academy office to correct details that are wrong.

Certificates

Graduation certificates are generated from the information in the portal at the moment they are downloaded. Check the student record before a certificate is issued.

Availability

The academy may take the portal offline for maintenance or changes. The portal is not an emergency channel; for urgent matters, call the academy office.

Changes to these terms

The academy may update these terms as the portal changes. The date at the top of this page shows the current version.

Contact

Questions about these terms can go to the academy office at office@academy.edu or on +91 80 4000 1010.
